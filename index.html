00 · The Problem

Most companies don't fail at AI because the tech is bad. They fail because nobody makes them commit.

Pilots stall. Champions leave. The subscription renews itself for a tool three people opened once. Six months later, the budget line is still there and the outcome isn't — and the business right next to you already shipped.

The abandonment problem

77%

of SMBs that buy an AI tool abandon it within six months. Not because the model got worse — because nobody built it into how the business actually runs.

The invisibility problem

3M+

small businesses in the US have no real online presence for AI search or agents to find them. Every day that stays true, the next competitor to fix it takes the customer.

The window problem

18 months

is roughly how long before most SMBs will have locked in an AI partner for the next several years. The ones who choose well now compound the advantage. This isn't a "someday" decision anymore.

We've watched this pattern from both sides — as the team selling $100M of enterprise software, and as the team now auditing what got left unused inside it. The fix isn't more tooling. It's one team that scans what you already have, builds the agent that actually runs, and stays accountable to whether it worked.

12 · Good Questions

Answered straight, not spun.

Is the 15-minute call really free? Am I going to get upsold?
Free. No card, no deck. In 15 minutes we'll tell you whether Forge is the right team for your problem — and if it's not, we'll point you to who is. We've sent people to competitors when the fit wasn't ours.
I don't even know which Engine or service I need.
That's the most common starting point. You don't need to know — we ask four questions about your operation and can usually spot the bottleneck inside eight minutes. Diagnosis is the job.
Does Assay write to my Salesforce org?
No. Assay uses read-only OAuth and never writes to your org. Revoke access anytime from Salesforce Connected Apps.
How fast can an Engine actually go live?
A simple Engine — Care, Bernie — is typically 2–4 weeks. A complex one — an integrated Sales Engine, or a Bid Engine wired into procurement — is 6–8 weeks. Ship day is when it enters production running, not when training starts.
Do you work outside the US?
Yes. Delivery is trilingual from day one — English, Portuguese (BR), and Spanish — with clients across the US, Brazil, Mexico, and Argentina.
Do you offer ongoing managed services?
Yes, but never open-ended retainers that bill you to bill you. Ongoing work continues because it's still delivering measurable value — no lock-in, no auto-renewing dead weight. We earn the next month, every month.
We tried another AI vendor and it went badly. Why would this be different?
Most vendors hand over a tool and disappear. We hand over a running system — with on-call support, a kill switch, and 30 days of tuning included. If it breaks, we're in the project's Slack until it's fixed.
